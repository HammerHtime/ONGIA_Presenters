# Counting ONGIA's history (Andrew's rules)

How the year-over-year overview counts events and people from Wix: the Archive site (2021 to mid-2025), the
current site (mid-2025 on), and events run on the Briefing Room. Decided by Andrew, 1 Nov 2026.

## People
- **Attendee vs contact.** Someone who comes to three events in a year is **one contact** but **three attendees**:
  one at each event. "People trained" and every per-event figure count attendances; "contacts reached" and growth in
  the network count distinct people (matched by email address).
- **One row per person.** Wix lists a ticketed order's buyer and each ticket's holder; count the people, never both
  (`attendeesOf`). Within one event, the same email twice is one attendee.
- Figures are **registrations** unless the event was checked in on the Briefing Room. Say which.

## Events
- **A conference is one event.** In-person, overflow and virtual registrations for the same conference count as one
  event, and its attendees are all of them added together.
- **Virtual is counted twice over, on purpose:** in the event's total, and again in "trained virtually". Virtual
  attendance and webinars count as virtual (no city or province of delivery).
- **Meal add-ons are not training.** The 2022 conference lunch buffets are left out.
- **Edmundston, October 2026, is one training** (the 13 Oct and 15 Oct Wix events together).

## Places (Wix has these wrong)
- "Crime at the Crossroads (Winnipeg)", March 2026: **Winnipeg** (Wix says Kingston).
- "Street Gang Training Symposium – Montreal", April 2025: **Montreal** (Wix says Calgary).
